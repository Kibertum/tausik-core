## Goal

Determine from natural accepted work whether GPT-5.6/6 Luna can replace Terra for bounded routine tasks while holding quality and rework constant, then route only the proven class.

## Acceptance Criteria

Eligibility and escalation predicates are frozen before observations and exclude security, migrations, architecture, ambiguous scope, and prior quality failure. At least three natural routine tasks record model, effort, rounds, review/rework, verification and acceptance without paid or synthetic fan-out. Luna becomes the default only if every quality gate passes and accepted-task credit cost improves against the frozen comparable baseline; otherwise Terra remains default with the refusal recorded. No active task changes model identity and Sol/Astra escalation remains explicit.
