## Decision

1.11 planning refinement: owner asks to critically reduce self-development overhead and unnecessary tests. Test-suite audit moves directly after native Codex baseline, no longer waits for compound workflow. Use compact family-level removal evidence, risk-based scoped verification and meaningful failure detection; no deletion quota or ceremonial replacement tests. A bounded self-development exception must identify the specific check, scope, replacement evidence and expiry; this decision does NOT activate a blanket gate bypass. Preserve task/AC evidence, security, migration/restore and release acceptance. No automatic consumer-project policy change.

## Rationale

Self-hosting amplifies workflow cost. The framework must be revisable, but authoring it is not evidence that a check is unnecessary. Early small consolidation batches avoid spending the whole release auditing tests. Runtime savings, output/retry tokens and subscription quota are separate measurements.
