## Decision

SENAR v1.1 Essential adopted as core methodology for Frai. All SHALL requirements implemented. QG-0 and QG-2 are hard gates (CLI-enforced). Rules 9.1-9.4, 9.6 are instruction-based (acceptable for Essential tier). Rule 9.5 implemented as audit check/mark.

## Rationale

SENAR provides formalized quality control for AI-native development. Essential tier matches Frai's 1-2 Supervisor+AI Pair use case.
